## 0.5.0

- Portable CPU generation, adjacent-round neural fitting, candidate selection,
  selected-MAP evaluation, goal-conditioned predictive checks and frozen-network
  marginal calibration checks, with explicit configurations and restart support.
- Eight unchanged final neural estimators, compact numerical evidence, and
  commands rebuilding seven reference figures and fourteen CSV tables.
- Separate predictive and calibration reporting, including all parameter-wise
  calibration findings and the original-prior predictive comparison.
- Reader navigation, biological/parameter glossary, paper reproduction map,
  data-access policy and reference-source provenance.
- Independently verified plotting and full CPU installations on Linux with
  Python 3.8.20, constrained dependencies, eight-model loading and 60 unit checks.
- Training logs kept within each fit's output directory; reference-only scripts
  separated from the supported executable workflow.

Full scientific simulation/training campaigns were not repeated to verify the
software distribution. The [validation guide](docs/VALIDATION.md) describes the
checks actually executed and the production commands supplied for independent
regeneration.
