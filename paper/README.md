# Companion paper

**Bridging Optimization and AI for Parameterizing Spatial Stochastic Systems**
by Michael Alexander Ramirez Sierra and Thomas R. Sokolowski accompanies this
software. The manuscript is under revision at the European Journal of Applied
Mathematics; no journal DOI is assigned here.

The software's [citation metadata](../CITATION.cff) identifies the project and
authors. The [paper reproduction map](../docs/REPRODUCTION_MAP.md) connects its
scientific results to code, configurations and numerical evidence.

The underlying ICM model is described in
[PLOS Computational Biology (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1012473),
and the cavity extension in the
[doctoral thesis (2025)](https://doi.org/10.21248/gups.92001).
